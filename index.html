About me

Hi, I'm Rawan Wael Saber — known as Rawan Motawa. I'm a Software Tester specialized in Manual Testing, with hands-on experience in Automation Testing using Selenium with Java.

I've worked across e-commerce and healthcare platforms — writing detailed test cases, reporting bugs with clear reproduction steps, and making sure the final product feels reliable, fast, and easy to use.
